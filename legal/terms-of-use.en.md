## 2. What Kalia does

Kalia plans your meals for the week to suit your goal: losing weight, maintaining weight, building muscle, gaining weight or eating more balanced. It suggests dishes whose planned calories and protein per portion fit your targets, creates the matching shopping list and offers a short daily check-in.

Kalia is a planning tool. It is not a calorie tracker and does not record what you actually eat. All nutrition values in Kalia are planned estimates per portion. The actual values depend on the products, quantities and preparation you use.

## 3. AI-generated content

Transparency notice under the EU AI Act: Kalia uses artificial intelligence (Google Gemini) to create meal plans, recipes, ingredient lists, quantities and nutrition estimates. When you create a plan with Kalia+ or replace a dish, you are interacting indirectly with an AI system.

AI-generated content is not reviewed by people before it is shown in the app. It can contain errors, for example in quantities, nutrition values, cooking times or ingredients. Kalia checks AI results automatically for plausibility, but this does not replace your own judgment.

Dish photos are illustrations from an image library within the app. They may differ from the dish you cook. Some images in the app were created with AI tools.

## 4. Health notice

Kalia does not provide medical, dietary or nutritional advice and does not replace advice or treatment from a doctor or a qualified nutrition professional.

Your targets are estimated with a general formula from the details you enter. They are guidance values, not an individual medical recommendation. Kalia applies safety limits, but it cannot take your personal health situation into account.

Please talk to a doctor or a qualified nutrition professional before changing your diet if you:

- are pregnant or breastfeeding
- have a medical condition, for example diabetes or kidney disease
- take medication
- have or have had an eating disorder

Kalia is intended for adults and not for people under 18. Kalia does not promise any particular result, such as weight loss or muscle gain. If you feel unwell, stop following the plan and seek medical advice.

**Allergies and intolerances.** Kalia takes the allergies, intolerances and unwanted ingredients you enter into account when choosing dishes. A filter cannot replace checking for yourself, and AI content and recipe data can be wrong. If you have an allergy or intolerance, always check the ingredient list of the products you buy, including warnings about traces and cross-contamination.

**Food hygiene.** Follow the usual food hygiene rules, for example when handling raw meat, fish and eggs and when storing leftovers.

## 5. Portions for other people

If you add portions for people who eat with you, Kalia increases the quantities in the shopping list. Your targets do not change, and the nutrition values shown always refer to your own portion. Kalia does not take the needs of other people into account, in particular those of children, pregnant people or people with allergies.

We do not guarantee that a shopping trip will stay within a budget.

There is no business relationship, cooperation or partnership between Kalia and the supermarkets named in the app. Supermarket names and logos are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify the price region. Credits for the logos are listed in the app.

## 7. Check-in and streak

The daily check-in lets you note how the day went with your plan. Your streak counts the days with a completed check-in. Pause days can cover a missed day. Check-ins, streak and pause days are meant as motivation. They have no monetary value and give no claim to any reward. They are stored only on your device and can be lost if you delete the app or change your device.
